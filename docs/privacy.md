# xlatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

xlatch is a client for servers you control. The xlatch iOS app and share extension connect directly to servers that you pair with the app. byteowlz UG (haftungsbeschränkt) does not operate an xlatch relay and does not receive the content you share, capability inputs, results, server addresses, device keys, or usage history.

## Data stored on your device

xlatch stores paired-server configuration, cryptographic credentials, capability preferences, queued transfers, and activity needed to provide the features you use. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Shared app state and queued content are stored in the app's private container or App Group container. You can remove paired servers and queued or saved content from the app. Removing the app removes its local app data subject to iOS and iCloud backup behavior.

## Data sent to your servers

When you invoke an action or share content, xlatch sends the selected content and related request metadata directly to the xlatch server you chose. That server may store, transform, or forward the content according to its administrator's configuration. The operator of each paired server is responsible for that processing. Review the server configuration before sending sensitive information.

## Local-network access

xlatch requests local-network access so it can reach servers on your local network or private overlay network. Connections use HTTPS and a certificate pin established during pairing.

## Camera and Face ID

The camera is used only to scan pairing codes. Face ID can authorize security-sensitive approvals. xlatch receives only the success or failure result from iOS and does not receive or store biometric data.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

xlatch contains no third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. byteowlz does not sell personal data collected through xlatch.

## Changes

If xlatch later adds a hosted relay, push-notification service, analytics, or other byteowlz-operated service, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that feature is released.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the [xlatch support page](https://github.com/byteowlz/xlatch/blob/main/docs/support.md).
